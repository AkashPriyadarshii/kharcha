# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Be respectful and constructive. No harassment, demeaning language, or personal attacks. Assume good faith. Disagreements happen; argue about the code, not the person.

## Scope

Applies to all project spaces: issues, PRs, discussions, and the community around the repo.

## Enforcement

Maintainers review reports privately. Violations receive a warning or removal from threads, repeated violations lead to a block. Report issues to the maintainer via GitHub issues.
