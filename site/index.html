FAQ

Questions, answered

Is my data safe?

Yes. Kharcha is offline-first — your transactions live on your device, protected by app lock (biometric/PIN). Data is encrypted in transit and never sold, shared, or used for ad targeting.

Does it need SMS permission?

No. Kharcha uses notification access (the same channel your phone uses to display payment alerts), which is Play-safe and doesn't read your messages.

Which apps does it track?

GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, and any UPI/banking app whose payment notifications contain a ₹ amount.

Do I have to enter expenses manually?

No — that's the point. Payments appear automatically. Manual entry exists only as a fallback.

Will double notifications double my expenses?

Never. Every payment carries a unique UPI reference that Kharcha dedupes against, so each expense is counted exactly once.

Does it work offline?

Fully. Capture, categorization, budgets, and reports all work with zero network. Kharcha syncs to the cloud only as a backup when you're online.
